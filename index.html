About Me

I'm Danylo Tykhonov — a frontend developer who builds modern, fast, and responsive websites with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Whether it's a business landing page or a portfolio site, I approach every project with attention to detail and a focus on how the interface actually feels to use.

I'm constantly improving my skills through practice and real projects, so I can offer clients clean code, solid markup, and results that truly look and perform at a high level.
